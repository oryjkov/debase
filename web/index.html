DEBASE

Place an exit point on Swiss terrain and debase flies a point-mass wingsuit model out along every heading, judging how much air each one leaves you against swisstopo's 0.5 m elevation data.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no backend and no account: exits, parameters and any FlySight track you load stay on this machine, and the only thing that leaves it is a request for public map tiles. Coverage is Switzerland only — that is where the elevation data ends.

Source

github.com/oryjkov/debase — the code, the method, and the reasoning behind the numbers.

Data & libraries

  • swissALTI3D — the elevation model every number is measured from, read straight from the published cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs (0.5 m near the exit, 2 m further out). © swisstopo, open data.
  • swisstopo 3D terrain — the mesh you see and click on. It is a simplified rendering, not the analysed surface. © swisstopo.
  • SWISSIMAGE and swisstopo colour map — the satellite and map layers, via the public WMTS service. © swisstopo.
  • CesiumJS — globe rendering. Apache-2.0.
  • geotiff.js — reads the elevation rasters by HTTP range request. MIT.

Disclaimer

This is a planning aid, not a go/no-go authority.

  • The terrain you see is not the terrain that is measured. The rendered mesh is smoothed and simplified; the verdicts come from elevation rasters captured by a periodic aerial survey, which is not a current picture of the rock.
  • That data carries roughly half a metre of positional uncertainty. Against near-vertical rock, half a metre is tens of metres of apparent clearance.
  • Verdicts judge the first 500 m of descent and nothing deeper. A clear heading says nothing about what waits below that.
  • The flight model is a fit, not your flight. Real glide moves with suit, body position, temperature, altitude and the day.
  • Nothing here models wind, turbulence, rotor, cables, wires, trees, snow, other traffic, access, legality, or anything else absent from an elevation raster.

Verify everything on the ground, with your own eyes and your own judgement. Wingsuit BASE jumping can kill you, and a green sector on this page changes nothing about that. If you step off a cliff on the strength of what you read here, the decision and the risk are entirely yours. No warranty of any kind.
